Grammar guide

Reported Speech: Rules & Forms

Learn how to report statements, questions, commands and requests. Pay attention to changes in verb tenses, pronouns and time expressions.

What is Reported Speech?

Reported Speech is used when we tell someone what another person said without repeating their exact words.
Direct Speech: Tom said, "I am tired."
Reported Speech: Tom said that he was tired.
We usually change the tense when the reporting verb is in the past (said, told, asked).
01 · Tense changes

How do tenses change?

🌱

Present Simple → Past Simple

Используем Past Simple вместо Present Simple.

Direct: "I work in London."
Reported: He said that he worked in London.
🌿

Present Continuous → Past Continuous

Меняем am/is/are + V-ing на was/were + V-ing.

Direct: "I am studying."
Reported: She said that she was studying.
🍃

Present Perfect → Past Perfect

Меняем have/has + V3 на had + V3.

Direct: "I have finished."
Reported: He said that he had finished.
🌳

Present Perfect Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous

Меняем have/has been + V-ing на had been + V-ing.

Direct: "I have been waiting."
Reported: She said that she had been waiting.
🌼

Past Simple → Past Perfect

Обычно используем Past Perfect, чтобы показать, что действие произошло раньше.

Direct: "I bought a car."
Reported: He said that he had bought a car.
🌾

Past Continuous → Past Perfect Continuous

Возможен переход, если важно подчеркнуть длительность действия до момента речи.

Direct: "I was working all day."
Reported: She said that she had been working all day.
🌿

Past Perfect → Past Perfect

Past Perfect обычно не меняется.

Direct: "I had seen the film."
Reported: He said that he had seen the film.
☘️

Future forms

Will обычно меняется на would.

Direct: "I will call you."
Reported: She said that she would call me.
Future Continuous → would be + V-ing
Future Perfect → would have + V3

03 · Modal verbs

How do modal verbs change?

Can → Could

"I can swim."
He said that he could swim.

May → Might

"I may be late."
She said that she might be late.

Will → Would

"I will help you."
He said that he would help me.

Must → Had to

"I must leave."
She said that she had to leave.

Обычно при выражении обязанности.

Could → Could

"I could help."
He said that he could help.

Should → Should

"You should rest."
She said that I should rest.

Would → Would

"I would like some tea."
He said that he would like some tea.

Might → Might

"I might go out."
She said that she might go out.

Time and place expressions

here there

These changes depend on the context. If the place or time has not changed, the original expression may remain.
05 · Sentence structures

How to report different types of sentences

1. Statements

said (that) + sentence

Direct: "I am happy."
Reported: She said (that) she was happy.

После said можно использовать that или опустить его.

2. Statements with told

told + object + (that) + sentence

Direct: "I need help."
Reported: He told me (that) he needed help.

После told обязательно указываем, кому сказали.

3. Yes / No questions

asked + if / whether + subject + verb

Direct: "Do you like music?"
Reported: She asked me if I liked music.

4. Wh- questions

asked + question word + subject + verb

Direct: "Where do you live?"
Reported: He asked me where I lived.

Не используем порядок слов вопросительного предложения.

5. Commands

told + object + to + verb

Direct: "Close the door!"
Reported: He told me to close the door.

6. Negative commands

told + object + not to + verb

Direct: "Don't touch it!"
Reported: She told me not to touch it.

7. Requests

asked + object + to + verb

Direct: "Please help me."
Reported: He asked me to help him.

8. Suggestions

suggested + -ing

Direct: "Let's go out."
Reported: She suggested going out.
06 · When don't we change the tense?

Cases when backshift is not necessary

1. Reporting verb in the present

Direct: "I live in Berlin."
Reported: He says that he lives in Berlin.

2. General truths and facts

Direct: "Water boils at 100°C."
Reported: The teacher said that water boils at 100°C.

3. Something that is still true

Direct: "I work at a hospital."
Reported: She said that she works at a hospital.

Если информация по-прежнему актуальна, можно оставить настоящее время.

Remember: Backshift is common when the reporting verb is in the past, but it is not automatic in every situation. The speaker's meaning and the context are important.
07 · Pronoun changes

Don't forget to change pronouns!

Direct: Anna said, "I love my family."
Reported: Anna said that she loved her family.
Direct: Peter said to me, "You can use my computer."
Reported: Peter told me that I could use his computer.

Pronouns change depending on who is speaking, who is listening and who is reporting the information.

Part 2 · Grammar

Reported Speech · B1

Statements, questions, commands and requests.

Grammar reminder

Direct speech: Tom said, "I am tired."
Reported speech: Tom said that he was tired.
Direct question: "Where do you live?"
Reported question: She asked me where I lived.
Command: "Close the door."
Reported command: He told me to close the door.
Remember:
  • Present Simple → Past Simple
  • Present Continuous → Past Continuous
  • Past Simple → Past Perfect
  • Will → Would
  • Can → Could
  • Questions use statement word order.
  • Commands: told / asked + person + to-infinitive.
